# Set B: 100 movie-inspired 9-to-5 escapes (131-230)

Inspired by 1990s and early-2000s films about hating the day job. The film is a reference note only: prompts never name films, characters, quotes or logos, and people stay off-screen.

### 131. Cover-sheet memo *(Office Space (1999))*

- **Start:** A single report on a desk with a missing cover sheet and one memo clipped to it.
- **Workplace disaster:** Duplicate reminder memos arrive by tube, by tray and by pin, each clipping to the report until it is buried in identical memos.
- **Carnival:** A carousel of identical memos riding wooden-style frosted horses around the report on a pole.
- **Apocalypse:** A drift of memos entombs a ruined cubicle row; a lime binder clamps the single report with its cover sheet.

### 132. Exiled stapler *(Office Space (1999))*

- **Start:** A lime stapler on a tidy desk, its place outlined on the blotter.
- **Workplace disaster:** The stapler is moved desk by desk down a corridor, then down a stairwell into a dim basement storage room.
- **Carnival:** A descending spiral slide carrying the stapler down past every floor into the basement.
- **Apocalypse:** A flooded basement ruin full of relocated desk objects; a lime lift carries the stapler back up.

### 133. Printer taken to the field *(Office Space (1999))*

- **Start:** A printer on a stand showing a paper-jam error light, one crumpled sheet in its tray.
- **Workplace disaster:** The printer is wheeled out of the office on a cart, across a car park and into an open grass field.
- **Carnival:** A strongman high-striker attraction where the printer sits on the target plate under the hammer.
- **Apocalypse:** Printer fragments scattered across a scorched field; a lime repair rig reassembles a working paper path.
